# Health OS Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 12 September 2026**

Health OS is a personal health data ingestion and analytics application currently operated for private, non-commercial use.

## Data Access

Health OS may access health and fitness information authorized by the user through connected services such as WHOOP. This may include recovery, sleep, workout, cycle, profile, and body measurement data.

## Use of Data

Data accessed by Health OS is used solely to provide personal health tracking, analytics, and related Health OS functionality.

Health OS does not sell personal data or use personal health data for advertising.

## Data Sharing

Health OS does not intentionally disclose personal health data to third parties except where necessary to operate services explicitly connected and authorized by the user.

## Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and API tokens are not published in the Health OS public repository. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and health data.

## Revoking Access

Users may revoke Health OS access to connected services through the relevant service provider where supported.

## Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the application contact listed with the connected service provider.
